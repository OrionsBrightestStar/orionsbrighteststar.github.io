2. Homotopy III

Based on the Reparametrization Lemma from yesterday's blog entry, we obtain the following proposition:

Proposition 1

We necessarily have that $F * C \simeq F \text{ rel } X \times \partial I$, and we also have $C *F \simeq F \text{ rel }X \times \partial I$

Proof.

Using the Reparametrization Lemma, for the first case, let $\phi_1(t)=2t$ when $t\leq 1/2$ and 1 when $t \geq 1/2$, and $\phi_2(t)=t$, and for the second case, let $\phi_1(t)=0$ when $t\leq 1/2$, and $2t-1$ when $t\geq 1/2$, and then $\phi_2(t)=t$.

We can also define an "inverse homotopy," which essentially reverses the homotopy in question, like so:

Definition 1

Suppose we have a homotopy $F: X \times I \to Y$. Define $F^{-1}:X \times I \to Y$ where $F^{-1}(x,t)=F(x,1-t)$.
