Introductory Abstract Algebra

Algebra · Expository Guide

This is a work in progress of my notes on abstract algebra at the undergraduate level. I am closely following Artin's treatment of algebra, using Dummit and Foote's Abstract Algebra and Lang's Algebra as references as well.

It should be noted that these notes are written in conjunction with another set of abstract algebra notes, and are collectively being posted on the Instagram eigenvex. Be sure to check those notes out as well if you are also learning abstract algebra, and feel free to give that Instagram account a follow!
