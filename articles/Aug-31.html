An Introduction to Differential Forms

Geometry & Topology

1. Introduction

Differential forms provide a powerful language for studying geometry and analysis on smooth manifolds.

Let $M$ be a smooth manifold. A differential $k$-form on $M$ is a smooth section of $\Lambda^k(T^*M)$.

Definition 1.1 (Differential Form)

A differential $k$-form on a smooth manifold $M$ is a smooth section of the bundle $\Lambda^k(T^*M)$.

2. The Exterior Derivative

The exterior derivative is an operator $$ d:\Omega^k(M)\longrightarrow\Omega^{k+1}(M) $$ satisfying $d^2=0$.

Theorem 2.1

For every differential form $\omega$, $$ d(d\omega)=0. $$

Proof.

This follows directly from the defining properties of the exterior derivative.
