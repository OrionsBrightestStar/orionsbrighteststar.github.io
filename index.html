Theorem 1.1 (Fundamental Theorem of Calculus)

Let $f:[a,b]\to\mathbb{R}$ be continuous and define $$ F(x)=\int_a^x f(t)\,dt. $$ Then $F$ is differentiable on $(a,b)$ and $$ F'(x)=f(x). $$
